Before you contact us

For faster troubleshooting, include:

  • App: 687 Italy
  • Device and operating system
  • Browser or app version
  • Which screen/function has the problem
  • What you tapped immediately before the problem
  • A screenshot, if possible

Do not email passwords, payment-card numbers or unnecessary identity documents.

Common checks

User Guide does not open

Open /687-italy-user-guide.pdf from the same 687 Italy site. If it opens there, return to Settings and try User Guide again.

Location / Nearby / NOW

Confirm that Location permission is allowed for the 687 Italy website/app and that location services are enabled on the device.

Voice Translate

Confirm microphone permission and select the language you are speaking before starting voice input.

Guardian notifications

Guardian must be armed, notification permission must be granted, and the relevant Guardian preference must be ON.

External booking or navigation

External providers can change their websites and may require you to confirm or re-enter details. Bookings and navigation are handled by the external provider.

Emergency help

687 Support is not an emergency service.

In Italy, call 112 for emergency assistance and follow official local instructions.
