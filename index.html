How TripItaly works

TripItaly focuses on planning and travel assistance. Commercial bookings are completed with external travel partners.

1

Plan your trip

2

3

Book with trusted partners

When you choose a travel service, TripItaly can redirect you to an external booking provider to complete the purchase.
