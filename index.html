WHAT MAKES 687 DIFFERENT

More than an itinerary. A travel companion that reacts to your day.

The core of 687 is not a list of attractions. It is an intelligent layer that connects your itinerary, your location, the time, local conditions and the next practical decision.

🧠
INTELLIGENT DAY

Your day, interpreted as it happens.

Intelligent Day brings together Today, NOW, Budget, Prevent, Guardian, Signals and Rescue. Instead of opening several disconnected tools, you see the most useful travel context around the day you actually have.

  • Understand what still fits into your day
  • Connect itinerary, timing and current location
  • Adapt when the original plan changes
🛡️
GUARDIAN

Important travel changes should find you.

Guardian is designed to keep relevant trip signals visible while you travel. It works as the protective layer around the day, helping surface timing, route and travel-context changes that deserve attention.

  • Keep important signals close
  • Reduce the need to check several services manually
  • Stay aware while moving between stops
🌦️
WEATHER + PREVENT

Weather is useful only when it changes a decision.

687 does not treat weather as a decorative forecast. Prevent puts conditions such as rain, heat, visibility and other practical context next to the travel day so you can change the plan before it becomes a problem.

  • See conditions in travel context
  • Adjust timing or activities earlier
  • Use weather as a decision tool, not just a forecast
TRIP PLANNING

Plan the whole trip — not only what happens after you arrive.

687 brings the main parts of a real journey into the same travel plan. Start with how you get there, connect the cities you want to visit, organize where you stay and protect the bookings or goals that cannot move.

✈️

International flights

Organize the flights that start and finish the trip, with arrival and departure times connected to the itinerary instead of sitting in a separate note.

🛫

Domestic flights

When the destination is large, add internal flights between regions or cities and account for airport transfers and travel time inside the plan.

🚆

Trains & transfers

Plan rail journeys and other city-to-city connections so the route between destinations becomes part of the itinerary, not an afterthought.

🏨

Hotels & stays

Keep accommodation linked to each city and stage of the journey, making it easier to understand where each travel day starts and ends.

🏙️

Multi-city travel

Build a journey across several cities, reorder stops and see the trip as one connected route rather than a collection of unrelated days.

🎟️

Bookings & fixed times

Add reservations, timed entries, tours and other commitments that must happen at a specific time, then build the rest of the day around them.

🎯

Travel goals & must-see places

Mark the places and experiences that matter most so they stay visible while the itinerary is built and adapted during the trip.

🚗

Car rental & road days

Include rental-car days, driving stages and road stops, then move from the plan to supported navigation when it is time to go.

FAQ

Questions about 687.

Is 687 one app for every country?

687 is a family of country-focused apps. The core travel system stays familiar while local information and tools adapt to each destination.

What is Intelligent Day?

It is the daily travel layer that connects Today, NOW, Budget, Prevent, Guardian, Signals and Rescue so you can understand the day and adapt it without starting again from zero.

What is Guardian?

Guardian is designed to keep important travel context and changes visible while you are moving, so you do not need to constantly check several disconnected services.

Does 687 replace Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze?

No. 687 helps you decide where you need to go and then works with supported navigation services for the actual route.

Where do I get a 687 app?

Open the page for the destination you want. Each developed product page contains its official store area for App Store and Google Play availability.

What is 687 Travel?

687 is a family of country-focused travel companion apps. Each edition combines trip planning, daily travel tools and destination-specific context in one familiar system.

Can I plan flights, trains and hotels with 687?

687 helps you organize the transport and stays that shape the trip, including international flights, domestic connections, trains, hotels and transfers, so they remain connected to the itinerary.

Can I build a multi-city itinerary?

Yes. 687 is designed for trips that move through several cities, with stops, stays, transfers, bookings and travel goals connected inside one route.

Can I add bookings and must-see places?

Yes. Timed reservations, tours, fixed commitments and important travel goals can be kept visible while you build and adapt the itinerary.

Does 687 help with car rental and road travel?

687 can include car-rental days and road stages in the trip structure, then connect the plan to supported navigation services when it is time to drive.

Does 687 still help after I arrive?

Yes. The planning layer remains useful during the trip, while tools such as Intelligent Day, NOW, Nearby, Prevent, Guardian, Budget, translation and navigation help with day-to-day decisions.
