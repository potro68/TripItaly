Privacy information for 687 Italy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026.

687 Italy is a travel-planning app designed to help users organize a trip to Italy before and during travel.

Information stored on your device

  • Trip details such as dates, route, cities, budget and planned expenses.
  • Travel Wallet documents you choose to add.
  • App preferences and saved/recoverable trip data.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device. You can remove local trip data from the app's Tools section.

Location

With your permission, 687 Italy may use your device location for features such as GPS verification, Nearby places, weather, maps and navigation. Location access is controlled by your device settings.

Microphone and speech

If you use Translate voice input, microphone and speech-recognition access may be used to transcribe the words you choose to speak. Permissions remain under your device control.

External services

687 Italy can open third-party services for travel searches, booking, maps, weather, nearby places and other travel functions. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their own terms.

Bookings and payments

687 Italy does not process travel payments or store payment-card details. Purchases are completed directly with external providers.

Affiliate links

Some external travel links may be affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase, 687 may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Children

687 Italy is a general travel utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The current version will be published on go687.com.
