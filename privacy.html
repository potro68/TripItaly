PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how 687 Italy handles information when you plan and use a trip. 687 Italy is part of the 687 travel network. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@go687.com.

1. Information used by the app

Trip and budget data

The app may store trip dates, cities, itinerary items, travel preferences, home currency, budget amounts, expenses, accommodation details and other planning information you enter.

Location

Live features such as Nearby, NOW, navigation, Prevent, Signals and Guardian may request your device location. Location access is permission-based. In production, Italy live features are intended for use in Italy.

Microphone and translation

If you use voice input, your device/browser speech service may process microphone input. Text submitted for translation may be sent to the translation service used by the app.

Notifications

If you enable Guardian notifications, the app may register a push subscription and send a limited live context to 687 Pulse so relevant alerts can be generated.

2. 687 Pulse, Signals and Guardian

Signals uses an anonymous persistent device identifier so reports and votes can work without a public profile. A report can include its type, approximate location, timestamp and the selected route/stop context needed by the feature.

Guardian can sync a compact context such as current coordinates, locality, current trip day, next unfinished stop, an optional fixed commitment, notification preferences and timestamps. This is used to evaluate weather, leave-time, route and important-signal alerts. Old context is not used indefinitely for live alert decisions.

Nearby travelers may see relevant active Signals. The app is designed not to publish your hotel address, identity documents or live GPS trail as a public profile.

3. Information kept on your device

Much of the trip state is stored locally in browser/app storage so your itinerary continues to work. Travel Wallet files such as booking confirmations or vouchers are stored locally on the device by the current app implementation. Do not store credit-card data or identity documents in Travel Wallet.

4. External services

687 Italy relies on third-party services for selected functions, which may receive the minimum request data needed for that function. These may include mapping/navigation, nearby-place search, translation, weather/timezone data, reverse geocoding, exchange-rate reference data, flight/travel search and booking partners.

When you open Google Maps, Waze or another external travel provider, you leave 687 Italy and that provider's own privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Permissions

  • Location: used only for features that need your current position.
  • Microphone: used when you choose voice translation/input.
  • Notifications: used when you enable Guardian alerts.
  • Local file access: used when you choose to add supported travel documents to the local Wallet.

You can deny or revoke device permissions in your browser or operating-system settings. Some features will then be unavailable.

6. Your choices and requests

You can remove itinerary items, expenses, local Wallet documents and archived trips through the app where controls are provided. Clearing the app/site data from your device can also remove locally stored app state.

For questions about backend data associated with 687 Pulse, privacy requests or deletion requests, contact support@go687.com. We may need technical details such as the app and device/session identifier to locate anonymous operational records.

7. Children, security and changes

687 Italy is a general travel-planning service and is not designed to collect sensitive personal profiles from children. Use reasonable care when entering travel information and do not enter unnecessary sensitive information.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The policy may be updated when features, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the bottom of this page identifies the current version.
